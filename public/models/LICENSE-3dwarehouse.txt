Car models car_*.glb are free models from Trimble 3D Warehouse (https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com),
used under the 3D Warehouse General Model License. They were simplified (interior removed,
reduced to ~25-30k triangles), recentred and compressed for Arab RP. Real brand names are not
used in the game; each car has its own in-game name.
